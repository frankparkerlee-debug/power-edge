## Channels & cadence
- **Indeed** (paid post, ~$10–15/day) — where commission roofing reps actually search. Post #1.
- **Facebook**: roofing-sales groups (Roofing Sales Pros, D2D sales groups, DFW job boards) — post #2 weekly; Meta paid recruiting campaign runs alongside (built in Ads Manager).
- **Direct outreach**: LinkedIn/IG/FB DMs with #3–4. Ten DMs a day beats any job board for quality.
- **Events**: Win The Storm, RoofCon — collect numbers, text the careers link.
- **Timing**: push hardest right after big hail events, when other shops fumble pay and production.

## Compliance notes
- Reps are 1099 independent contractors — never promise wages/guaranteed income in posts (floor = per-approved-roof, contingent on production).
- Don't solicit a competitor's CUSTOMER lists or induce breach of enforceable non-competes; have each hire confirm they're unencumbered (counsel).
- Meta/Indeed ads run under EMPLOYMENT rules — no age/gender/zip micro-targeting (already handled in the campaign build).
