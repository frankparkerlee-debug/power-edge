Where the damage is.

11,856 verified storm-hit addresses, every one traced to a National Weather Service hail report. 213 have a phone number that is not DNC-flagged.

Read this before you build a schedule. DFW has had no qualifying hail since September 27. Nobody is chasing fresh damage, and that is the opportunity: these are homes hit between April and September that still have not been inspected, and Texas policies put a filing deadline on the date of loss, some as short as a year. You are not early. You are the last call before the window shuts.
This is a door operation, not a call floor. Only 213 of 11,856 addresses are legally dialable after the DNC scrub, under a day of work for one setter. The other 11,643 are door-knock only. Staff accordingly, and buy skip-trace against the big April storm days before hiring a second phone.

How to work it

  • Knockers: Fort Worth, Rhome and Benbrook off April 26 (up to 3″), then the April 28 belt through Johnson County, Rockwall, Royse City, Southlake and Keller.
  • Setters: Fort Worth (93), Dallas County (45), Commerce (33), Azle (22), Ennis (8), Benbrook (5). That is the entire dialable list. Burn it, then knock.
  • Always open with the date and the stone size for that address. Pull it from the tool. “Storms in your area” is a telemarketer. “Two and a half inch hail on April 26” is a neighbor.
  • Mark every door in the tool the same day. One tap, and it carries your name.

Territory

One city, one rep. The office assigns areas at /admin/territories; you open the knock tool as /admin/storms?key=…&rep=YourName and it lands on your own cities. If you are seeing the whole metroplex, your territory is not assigned yet. Call the office before you knock.
