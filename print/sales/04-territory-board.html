Where the damage is.

11,857 verified storm-hit addresses, every one traced to a National Weather Service hail report. 213 have a phone number that is not DNC-flagged.

Read this before you build a schedule. DFW has had no qualifying hail since September 27. Nobody is chasing fresh damage, and that is the opportunity: these are homes hit between April and September that still have not been inspected, and Texas policies put a filing deadline on the date of loss, some as short as a year. You are not early. You are the last call before the window shuts.
This is a door operation, not a call floor. Only 213 of 11,857 addresses are legally dialable after the DNC scrub, under a day of work for one setter. The other 11,644 are door-knock only. Staff accordingly, and buy skip-trace against the big April storm days before hiring a second phone.

How to work it

  • Knockers: Fort Worth, Rhome, Benbrook and Azle off April 25 (up to 3″), Arlington and Dallas County off April 26, Rockwall County, Quinlan and Commerce off April 27, then the April 28 belt through Johnson County, Southlake and Keller.
  • Setters: Fort Worth (93), Dallas County (45), Commerce (33), Azle (22), Ennis (8), Benbrook (5). That is the entire dialable list. Burn it, then knock.
  • Always open with the date and the stone size for that address. Pull it from the tool. “Storms in your area” is a telemarketer. “Two and a half inch hail on April 25” is a neighbor.
  • Mark every door in the tool the same day. One tap, and it carries your name.
