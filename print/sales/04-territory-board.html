Where the damage is.

11,856 verified storm-hit addresses, every one traced to a National Weather Service hail report. 213 of them have a phone number that is not DNC-flagged.

Read this before you build a schedule. DFW has had no qualifying hail since September 27. Nobody is chasing fresh damage right now, and that is the opportunity: these are homes hit between April and September that still have not been inspected, and most Texas policies put a filing deadline on the date of loss. You are not early. You are the last call before the window shuts.
This is a door operation, not a call floor. Only 213 of 11,856 addresses are legally dialable after the DNC scrub, under a day of work for one setter. The other 11,643 are door-knock only. Staff accordingly, and put skip-trace budget against the biggest storm days before hiring a second phone.

How to work it

  • Knockers start here: Fort Worth, Arlington, and Benbrook off April 26 (up to 3″), then the April 28 belt: Rockwall, Royse City, Southlake, Keller, Hurst, Richardson, Rowlett.
  • Setters start here: Fort Worth (93 dialable), Dallas County (45), Commerce (33), Azle (22). That is the whole list. Burn it, then knock.
  • Always open with the date and the stone size for that address. Pull it from the tool. “Storms in your area” is a telemarketer. “Two and a half inch hail on April 26” is a neighbor.
  • Mark every outcome in the tool the same day, or your teammate knocks the same door next week. That is how a company earns a reputation it doesn't want.
