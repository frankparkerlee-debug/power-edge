1. What PowerEdge will do

PowerEdge will inspect and photograph the roof and related components at no charge, provide a written assessment of observed conditions, and, where the homeowner elects to pursue an insurance claim, attend the carrier’s adjuster appointment to present its documentation, measurements, and estimate.

2. What PowerEdge will not do

PowerEdge is a contractor, not a public insurance adjuster. Consistent with Texas Insurance Code § 4102.163, PowerEdge will not file the homeowner’s claim, negotiate or adjust the claim on the homeowner’s behalf, interpret policy coverage, or advise the homeowner on the value of the claim. All coverage decisions rest with the homeowner and the homeowner’s carrier.

3. Scope, price, and when work begins

No repair or replacement work is authorized by this agreement. Work begins only under a separate written scope and price, signed by the homeowner, after the carrier has issued its scope of loss where a claim is involved. The homeowner understands that work performed before the adjuster inspects may not be covered.

4. Deductible

TEXAS LAW REQUIRES A PERSON INSURED UNDER A PROPERTY INSURANCE POLICY TO PAY ANY DEDUCTIBLE APPLICABLE TO A CLAIM MADE UNDER THE POLICY. IT IS A VIOLATION OF TEXAS LAW FOR A SELLER OF GOODS OR SERVICES WHO REASONABLY EXPECTS TO BE PAID WHOLLY OR PARTLY FROM THE PROCEEDS OF A PROPERTY INSURANCE CLAIM TO KNOWINGLY ALLOW THE INSURED PERSON TO FAIL TO PAY, OR ASSIST THE INSURED PERSON’S FAILURE TO PAY, THE APPLICABLE INSURANCE DEDUCTIBLE.

6. Insurance and workmanship

PowerEdge carries $2,000,000 in commercial general liability coverage; a certificate is available on request. Completed workmanship is guaranteed for one year from substantial completion. Manufacturer warranties on materials pass through to the homeowner under the manufacturer’s own terms.

7. Payment

Where work proceeds under an insurance claim, amounts due are the carrier-approved scope plus any homeowner-approved upgrades, with the homeowner responsible for the deductible. Where work proceeds outside a claim, the separate written scope and price controls.
