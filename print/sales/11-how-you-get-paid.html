How you get paid · Agreement version 2026-11-12
Rev. Nov 2026

One number, written down, that nobody moves.

Everyone's pay starts from gross profit on the roof. The cost side is fixed at $275 a square and written into your agreement, so you can work out your own check before it lands.

Gross profit

Gross profit = everything PowerEdge collects for the roof − ($275 × squares installed)
Collected includes insurance payments, approved supplements and what the homeowner pays.

Example (an illustration, not a promise): a 28-square roof at $600 a square collects $16,800. Gross profit is $16,800 − (28 × $275) = $9,100.

The $50 held inspection

An inspection is held only when all three are true:

  1. The homeowner was there. The closer records this in the app; a roof looked at with nobody home doesn't count.
  2. The closer finished the inspection with photos in the PowerEdge app.
  3. It was the first inspection PowerEdge ran at that address.

You get $50 for it on the next weekly run. The Inspections tab shows each one you booked as held or not, and why.

It's an advance on that roof. If the roof is approved, the $50 comes off your commission on it: on the example, $50 already paid plus $1,315 later. If the roof isn't approved, you keep the $50.

When money lands

  1. Half of your commission when PowerEdge receives the first insurance check on the roof (or the first payment on a cash job).
  2. The other half when the roof is complete and PowerEdge has collected the balance.
  3. Everything earned that week goes out on the weekly commission run.

A roof is approved when the insurer (or the homeowner, on a cash job) has approved the scope and the roof is contracted with PowerEdge for installation. A signature alone isn't approval.

Closers: the ramp advance

$600 a week for your first 6 weeks ($3,600 at most), as an advance against commission. Your first commissions repay it. Have 4 approved roofs by the end of week 8 and whatever is left is forgiven. If you leave before it's repaid, you owe nothing: it's recovered only from commissions, never from you personally.

Chargebacks

If a roof is canceled, isn't built, or PowerEdge isn't paid in full, commission paid on it wasn't earned. It comes off your future commissions. It's a correction, not a debt.

Credit and questions

  • The app's record of who booked each inspection and who closed each roof decides who is paid. Log every door.
  • Think it's wrong? Tell the office in writing within 30 days of the roof's approval.
  • You're a 1099 contractor: no taxes are withheld, and you'll get a Form 1099-NEC each January. Set money aside from every check.
