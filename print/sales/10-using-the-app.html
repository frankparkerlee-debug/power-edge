Using the app · Knockers and closers
Rev. Nov 2026

Your whole day is in one app.

Where to knock, who said what, every inspection you booked, and your numbers. If it isn't logged in the app, it didn't happen, and nobody can credit it to you.

Sign in

  • Go to poweredgetx.com/login and enter your work email. Tap the link in the email that arrives. No password.
  • You stay signed in for 30 days on that phone.
  • Put it on your home screen. iPhone: in Safari tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android: in Chrome tap the menu, then Add to Home screen.

The tabs

The map opens for knocking once you've finished Getting started: your details, the agreement, your W-9, direct deposit and the compliance check. City permits don't hold it up.

The knock map

  • Tap Near me, or type a ZIP. Switch Map and Aerial top right to see the roofs.
  • Pin colors: gray not knocked · yellow working · red said no · green signed or done · hollow ring not home · dark ring priority knock · blue roof permit in the last 10 years (Dallas), likely already replaced.
  • Doors are listed by street. Work a street end to end; the map fills in as you go.
  • Tap a house for Roof and storms (its hail history), Directions, and Call when there's a number that isn't on Do Not Call. "Knock only" means don't call it.

At each door

Every door gets one of four taps: Not home, Come back (set the time), Said no (pick the reason), or Book inspection.

Book inspection opens the closer windows that are free. Pick one with the homeowner, confirm their name and phone or email, leave the closer a note, done. The job, the closer, the homeowner's confirmation and the closer's email all happen from that one button.

After the booking

  • The house shows up in Inspections and Jobs. Follow it there; you don't need to call the office.
  • Closers: on the job, add your inspection photos and record what you found that day, including whether the homeowner was there. Homeowner there, photos in, and PowerEdge's first inspection at that address: that's a held inspection, and it's how your knocker gets their $50.
  • Closers: write the agreement in the Agreement tab. The homeowner's email is required; it's how they get their copy and their cancellation notice.

Your numbers

The Scoreboard (in the menu under your initial) shows your numbers and your team's, ranked, for today, this week and this month. Knockers see doors, inspections set, sets per 100 doors, roofs and revenue. Closers also see close rate and profit per roof. The office emails each of you a scorecard every week.
