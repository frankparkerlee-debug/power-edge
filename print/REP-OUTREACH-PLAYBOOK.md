# PowerEdge Rep Outreach Playbook (for the assistant)

**The job:** send 10 direct messages a day to working roofing/solar/D2D sales reps in DFW, get interested ones on Parker's calendar. That's the whole job. 30 to 45 minutes daily.

**Why this works:** the best closers are employed and underpaid. They don't browse job ads. A personal message with proof beats every ad channel we run.

---

## Daily workflow (30 to 45 min)

1. **Build today's list of 10** (15 min) — see "Where to find targets" below. Log each name in the tracker before messaging.
2. **Send 10 first-touch DMs** (15 min) — copy the script for the right platform, personalize the first line, attach the two screenshots.
3. **Send follow-ups** (5 min) — anyone from 3+ days ago with no reply gets the one follow-up. Never more than one.
4. **Route replies** (5 min) — any positive reply gets the booking message and gets flagged to Parker the same day. Speed matters more than anything else in this playbook.

## Where to find targets

- **Facebook groups:** search "roofing sales," "roofing sales pros," "door to door sales," "D2D sales," "DFW jobs." Join with the PowerEdge page or a personal profile. Target = people ACTIVE in comments who mention selling roofs/solar/pest/alarms in Texas. Their comment quality is the filter: articulate + hungry = message them.
- **Instagram:** search hashtags #roofingsales #d2dsales #doortodoor #roofinglife + location DFW. Reps who post door-knocking content are ideal (they're proud of the craft).
- **LinkedIn:** search "roofing sales representative" + Dallas-Fort Worth, also "solar sales," "outside sales." Filter to currently employed at OTHER companies.
- **The jackpot target:** anyone whose profile says team lead / sales manager at a roofing or solar company. They get the LEADER script and an immediate flag to Parker regardless of reply.
- Keep a do-not-message list: no one at PowerEdge's active business partners, and never message the same person twice with the first-touch script.

## What to attach (both, every time)

1. Screenshot of the **storm tool knock cards** (Parker will provide 2 to 3 approved screenshots — owner names blurred).
2. Screenshot of the **house dossier** (satellite roof view with squares).
These two images do the selling. The message just opens the door.

## Scripts

### First touch — rep (Facebook/Instagram)
> Hey [name], saw your post in [group] — you clearly know how to close. Quick one: we're PowerEdge, a DFW storm-restoration shop, and we built something no other shop here has. Storm data maps hail street by street, gives our reps the homeowner's NAME at every door in the path, and measures the roof from satellite while you're on the porch (screenshots attached).
>
> Comp is real too: 50% of gross profit against a FIXED cost basis in writing (no overhead skim, no moving redline), 55% from your 6th roof each month, paid weekly at the first check. And switching shops won't cost you a month — proven closers get a $600/week ramp draw for 6 weeks, forgiven once you close 4 roofs.
>
> Worth a 10-minute call with our owner? No pressure either way.

### First touch — rep (LinkedIn, shorter)
> [Name] — we're PowerEdge (DFW storm restoration). We hand reps a storm tool no other shop has (hail mapped to the street, homeowner names, satellite roof measurement — screenshots attached) and pay 50% of GP against a fixed written cost basis, weekly, with a forgivable $600/wk ramp for proven closers. If your current shop is slow-paying or stacking reps on the same streets, worth 10 minutes with our owner?

### First touch — LEADER (team lead / sales manager)
> [Name] — quick one for someone running a team. We're PowerEdge, DFW storm restoration: 4 crews, ~100-roof monthly capacity, and a storm-targeting tool no shop here has (hail mapped street by street, homeowner names, satellite measurement — screenshots attached). We're hiring a sales leader to build the knock team from day one: base plus override on team gross profit, real input on comp and territory. If you've got a downline that trusts you and a shop that doesn't deserve you, our owner would like 15 minutes.

### The one follow-up (3+ days, no reply)
> [Name], one nudge and I'll leave you alone. The short version: fixed redline in writing, forgivable ramp draw, and a storm tool that hands you the doors hail actually hit. If the timing's wrong, no worries — if you're curious, 10 minutes: [Parker's calendar link or "text 469-277-9594"].

### Positive reply → booking
> Great — fastest way is a quick call with Parker (the owner). What works this week: [two time options]? Or text him directly at 469-277-9594 and mention I sent you.

## Rules

- Personalize the FIRST LINE of every message (reference their post/profile). Never send obviously copy-pasted openers.
- Never trash-talk their current company by name.
- Never promise income. The words are always "commission," "draw is an advance against commissions," "earnings depend on production."
- Anyone rude or uninterested: one polite close-out, add to do-not-message, move on.
- LEADER prospects and any reply mentioning "my team" go to Parker the same hour.

## Tracking (simple sheet, 6 columns)

Date | Name | Platform + profile link | Script used | Reply? | Outcome (booked / follow-up sent / dead)

Weekly scoreboard to Parker every Friday: sent / replies / calls booked. Target: 50 sent/week, 5+ replies, 2+ calls. If two weeks under that, the scripts get revised — tell Parker rather than grinding.

## What Parker owns (not the assistant)

- The 10-minute calls and everything after.
- Approving the tool screenshots used as attachments.
- The referral-bounty asks to crews (personal asks land better from the owner).
