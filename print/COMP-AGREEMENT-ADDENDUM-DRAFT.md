# Sales Representative Commission & Advance Addendum — DRAFT for counsel review

**Not legal advice.** This is a business-terms draft for [Power Edge, LLC]'s attorney to review, revise, and approve before use. It is written to reflect the compensation Parker has set and to keep the arrangement squarely within an independent-contractor (1099) framework. Bracketed items are for counsel/Parker to confirm.

This Addendum supplements the Independent Contractor Agreement ("Agreement") between Power Edge, LLC ("Company") and the sales representative ("Contractor"). If it conflicts with the Agreement, the Agreement's independent-contractor provisions control.

---

## 1. Independent contractor status (controlling)

Contractor is an independent contractor, not an employee. Nothing in this Addendum — including any Advance under Section 3 — creates an employment relationship, a wage, a salary, or guaranteed compensation. Specifically:

- Contractor controls their own hours, methods, routes, and means of generating sales; Company specifies desired results, not the manner of achieving them.
- Contractor supplies their own vehicle, phone, and tools, and bears their own business expenses.
- Contractor is responsible for all self-employment and income taxes; Company will report payments on IRS Form 1099-NEC and will not withhold taxes.
- Contractor is free to provide services to others and receives no employee benefits.
- All amounts payable are **commissions and recoverable advances against commissions**, never wages.

## 2. Commission

**2.1 Definition of Gross Profit.** For each approved roofing project sold by Contractor, "Gross Profit" means the total amount **collected** by Company for the project (including approved insurance proceeds and supplements, and any customer out-of-pocket amounts), less a **fixed cost basis of $275.00 per roofing square** installed. The $275/square figure is a fixed, Company-published cost basis used solely to calculate commission; it is not adjusted after a project is sold.

**2.2 Commission rates.**
- **Self-generated projects:** 50% of Gross Profit.
- **Company-provided/appointment projects:** 30% of Gross Profit.
- **Volume accelerator:** For self-generated approved projects, the rate increases to **55% of Gross Profit on the sixth (6th) and each subsequent self-generated approved project within the same calendar month.** The count resets on the first day of each calendar month.

**2.3 Per-project floor.** Every approved project pays Contractor no less than **$1,250** ("Floor"), even where the Gross Profit calculation would yield less. The Floor applies only to approved projects that proceed to installation.

**2.4 "Approved project."** A project is "approved" when the insurer (or, for retail/cash jobs, the customer) has approved the scope and the project is contracted for installation with Company. Commission is earned as set out in Section 2.5, not merely upon signing.

**2.5 Payment timing.** For each approved project, Company pays **50% of the calculated commission upon Company's receipt of the first insurance proceeds check** (or first customer payment on a retail job) for that project, and the remaining **50% upon project completion and Company's collection of the balance.** Company pays earned commissions on its regular weekly commission run.

**2.6 Chargebacks.** If a project cancels, is not built, or Company is not paid in full (including a returned or reversed payment), any commission advanced or paid on that project is not earned and is reversed/recouped against future commissions. This is a reconciliation of unearned commission, not a debt.

## 3. Ramp Advance (the "forgivable draw")

**3.1 Advance.** For the first six (6) weeks of Contractor's engagement, Company will pay Contractor a **weekly advance of $600 against future commissions** ("Advance"), for a maximum aggregate Advance of $3,600. The Advance is an advance against commissions Contractor is expected to earn — it is **not** salary, wages, a bonus, a loan bearing interest, or guaranteed compensation.

**3.2 Recovery.** Earned commissions are first applied to repay the outstanding Advance balance before any commission is paid out to Contractor, until the Advance balance is zero.

**3.3 Forgiveness.** If Contractor closes **four (4) or more approved projects on or before the end of week eight (8)** of the engagement, the entire outstanding Advance balance is **forgiven** and Contractor retains all earned commissions with no offset for the Advance. Company may, in its discretion, forgive the balance on comparable performance.

**3.4 Non-recourse.** Except by offset against commissions as provided above, the Advance is **non-recourse**: if Contractor's engagement ends with an unrecovered Advance balance, Contractor owes Company nothing and Company will not seek repayment of that balance from Contractor personally. The Advance is recovered solely from commissions or not at all.

**3.5 Eligibility.** The Advance is offered at Company's discretion to experienced sales representatives and is documented in Contractor's onboarding terms. It may be modified or discontinued for future contractors but will not be changed retroactively for a Contractor already receiving it.

## 4. Compliance (roofing / insurance)

Contractor will not: waive, rebate, or absorb any customer's insurance deductible; represent themselves as a public insurance adjuster; or negotiate, adjust, or file a claim on the customer's behalf. Contractor documents damage and coordinates Company's inspection with the customer's adjuster; the customer files and manages their own claim. Contractor complies with Texas Insurance Code Ch. 4102 and all applicable law.

## 5. Illustrative example (not a guarantee)

A typical DFW project of ~28 squares at ~$700/square supplemented insurance scope collects ~$19,600. Gross Profit = $19,600 − (28 × $275) = $11,900. Self-generated commission at 50% = ~$5,950. Figures are illustrative; actual commissions depend entirely on Contractor's production and each project's economics, and are not promised or guaranteed.
